Red Letter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Red Letter collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. What you write stays on your device.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no backend service, and no analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind. No information about you or your use of the app is transmitted anywhere.

How this is enforced, not just promised

On Android, the INTERNET permission is removed from the released app. The operating system itself refuses any network connection the app might attempt, so this is a property of the build rather than a policy that depends on our good behaviour. The app makes no network requests.

Where your calendar is stored

  • Entirely on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Encrypted at rest using XChaCha20-Poly1305.
  • The encryption key is generated on your device and held in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. It is never written to ordinary storage and never leaves the device.
  • Excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so no copy is made to a cloud account without your involvement.

Permissions, and why

  • Notifications — only if you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled locally by the operating system. There is no push server and no push token.
  • Calendar — only if you export a day to your device calendar, and only requested at that moment. On iOS the app requests write-only access, so it cannot read your calendar at all. Only the title, date and time of a day you explicitly exported are written. Notes are never copied.
  • Biometrics — only if you turn the app lock on. Authentication is performed by the operating system; the app never receives your fingerprint, face data or passcode.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or access to your photos or files, and those permissions are explicitly blocked in the app's manifest.

Links in your entries

If an entry contains a link, the app inspects it on your device to warn you about addresses that are disguised or use unsafe schemes. This check is performed entirely offline. Links are not sent to any reputation, malware-scanning or URL-checking service, because doing so would mean disclosing your private calendar links to a third party. Nothing is opened, fetched or previewed until you confirm it.

Backups you create

Exporting a backup produces an unencrypted JSON file, handed directly to your device's share sheet. From that point it is an ordinary file under your control, and its privacy depends on where you choose to put it. The temporary copy the app creates is deleted immediately afterwards.

Children

Red Letter is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information at all, it collects none from children.

Your rights

There is no data held about you to access, correct, export or delete, because none is collected. Your own calendar data can be exported at any time from Settings, and "Delete everything" removes your entries, the encryption key and all preferences from the device permanently.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date. If a future version of the app ever collects anything, that change will be described here before it ships.
